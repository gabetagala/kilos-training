Two homes, same content. POSTER is the reference applied literally — gorgeous, and white-on-magenta measures 3.6:1, which is under the AA floor for small text. That's survivable on a screen she reads once, standing still. DARK is the one she reads mid-set, sweaty, at arm's length. The recommendation is both: the field owns the moments that are meant to be looked at — icon, home hero, finish — and the plum-dark owns the working screens. The player then bridges them, blooming into the full field on every lift and cooling back down on every lower.
