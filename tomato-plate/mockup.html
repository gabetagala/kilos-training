How to cut it
On the spoonFork-mashed, visible lumps
In his handsFinger-length baton, two fingers wide
Squash test. It must squash between your finger and thumb with light pressure. If it doesn't, cook it longer.
Prepare it
  1. Peel, cut into 2cm chunks.
  2. Steam 12–15 min until a fork meets no resistance.
  3. Keep the cooking water.
  4. Set aside a baton before you mash the rest.
  5. Mash, thinning with the cooking water.
Never thin with breastmilk on this plan — use the cooking water. Serve lukewarm; test on the inside of your wrist.
